Centri Drift

An arcade drifting game for one thumb. Hold to hook onto a tower, swing through the bend on the rope and let go at the right moment. The track never ends: twelve bends per world, then on to the next one, a little faster each time.

  • Endless course through changing worlds, from coast to volcano and city at night.
  • More than thirty cars to collect, paint and customize.
  • Daily gift, achievements and seasonal events.
  • Optional weekly leaderboards and an all-time top 100.
  • Optional account backup with Apple or Google to move your progress to a new device.
