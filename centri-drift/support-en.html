Move your progress to a new device

At first, your progress is stored only on your device. To take it with you, back up your account:

  1. On your current device, open SETTINGS → PROFILE → BACK UP ACCOUNT.
  2. Sign in with Apple or Google (depending on device and app version).
  3. Open Centri Drift on the new device, go to BACK UP ACCOUNT there too and sign in with the same Apple or Google account.

The backup covers coins, diamonds, cars and their customisations, avatars and frames, achievements, collectables, records, discovered worlds and your purchase entitlements. Sound, haptics and similar settings stay on each device.

If you have already played on both devices, the app shows both versions side by side and you pick one. The other is discarded — coins and cars are not added together. Choose carefully; this cannot be undone.

Changes are backed up when you are online and not driving, usually after you send the app to the background. Purchases and claimed rewards are backed up sooner.

Without an account backup we unfortunately cannot transfer or restore your progress, not even by e-mail: it exists only on your device. A device backup with Apple or Google may restore it along with your other data; we have no influence on that.

Restore purchases

Open the SHOP and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Permanent purchases such as REMOVE ADS are restored.

Diamond packs are consumables: the store does not provide them again. They are part of your balance, though, and come along if you have backed up your account (see above).

Purchase stuck at “AWAITING APPROVAL”? This happens, for example, when a parent still has to approve the purchase. As soon as it is approved, it arrives automatically.

Paid but received nothing? Restart the app with an internet connection; open purchases are then completed. If that doesn't help, write to us with the date, the product and the order number from the store's receipt. We don't need any payment details.

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us: for Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, for Google via the Google Play Help Center.

Delete your profile and account

In the app

Open SETTINGS → PROFILE → DELETE PROFILE and confirm. This deletes your runs, leaderboard entries, reward records, your player name, the account backup and your online account; a link to Apple or Google is removed. If your account is linked to Apple or Google, you will be asked to sign in once more to confirm.

What stays: your progress on this device, your purchases with the store and your Apple or Google account. In weekly and event groups that have already closed, the place, score and time remain without name or account ID so that other players' places do not shift. On the next start the app creates a new, empty profile; if you don't want that, switch off online leaderboards straight afterwards (see below). Details are in the privacy policy.

Important: deleting the app or switching off online leaderboards does not delete your online account.

Without the app, by e-mail

If you no longer have the app or it won't open, we delete your account on request:

  1. Write to inesgolubapps@gmail.com with the subject “Centri Drift – Delete profile”.
  2. Include your account ID if you have it (in the app under SETTINGS → PROFILE → COPY ACCOUNT ID); otherwise your player name and whether you played on iPhone or Android.
  3. If the account cannot be clearly matched to you, we will ask for further details, such as a best score or your cars. We never need a password or a code — please don't send anything like that.

We then delete the same data as DELETE PROFILE in the app and confirm by e-mail, within one month at the latest. We delete your e-mail itself once the request has been completed.

Linked with Apple? You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Centri Drift in your Apple ID settings under “Sign in with Apple”.

Switch off online leaderboards

Under SETTINGS → ONLINE LEADERBOARDS you switch off all online features: leaderboards, the weekly league, events and daily gifts. The game stays fully playable, just without rankings.

  • Your account remains and your entries are hidden: your name, avatar and frame disappear from the lists, and your place stays as an anonymous entry. Only DELETE PROFILE deletes your data. Details are in the privacy policy.
  • If you switch back on, you continue with the same account and your entries appear again.

Why doesn't my run count?

  • “OFFLINE · UNRANKED”: the run was started without an internet connection. To rank a run, the app obtains a permission before the start; without a connection that isn't possible.
  • “LEADERBOARD · UNRANKED”: the result could not be confirmed — for example because the connection dropped and the result was not transmitted in time, because the run lasted longer than two hours, or because it failed verification. The app resubmits results that don't go through immediately, but only for your most recent runs.
  • Online leaderboards are off: then nothing is ranked. Switch them on under SETTINGS → ONLINE LEADERBOARDS.
  • No new best: the leaderboard shows your best run. A weaker run still shows “SCORED” but does not change your place.
  • Event runs count towards the event leaderboard, not the regular one.
  • Not visible yet: weekly and event leaderboards can be up to ten minutes behind, the all-time TOP 100 up to 30 minutes.
  • New week: the weekly league changes on Mondays at 05:00 German time. A run after that already counts for the new week.

If you believe a run was wrongly left unranked, send us your account ID and the approximate date and time of the run.
